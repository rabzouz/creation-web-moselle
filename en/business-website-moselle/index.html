One-page or multi-page business website?

The one-page format

It suits a simple business with one main offer. All the information is organised on a single page: introduction, services, proof, indicative price and contact. It is a quick way to establish a professional presence.

The multi-page format

It becomes relevant when several services deserve a detailed explanation or when search rankings are an important lever. Each page then answers a specific intent and can evolve independently.
