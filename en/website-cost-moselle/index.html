What really changes the price

Two five-page websites can require very different amounts of work. The price depends less on a single figure than on the content and the level of customisation expected.

The number and depth of pages

A short introduction page does not require the same work as a complete service page with copy, FAQs, projects and calls to action.

The content available

If the copy, logo and photos are ready, production is more straightforward. When the message needs structuring, visuals need preparing or the identity needs creating, these services are included in the quote.

The features required

A simple form, a gallery, appointment booking or a catalogue do not involve the same complexity. Each feature must meet a real business need.

Simple advice

Start with the goal: being contacted, presenting several trades, taking bookings or growing your local visibility. The right format follows from that goal.

One-page or multi-page website?

Choose a one-page website if…

  • you are launching a business with one main offer;
  • you have little content;
  • your priority is to get a professional presence quickly.

Choose several pages if…

  • you offer several important services;
  • you want to target several Google searches;
  • you have projects, a team or a method to explain in detail.
